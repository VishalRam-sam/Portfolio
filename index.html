Pharma Sales & Inventory Analytics

Interactive Power BI (DAX) dashboard analyzing sales, inventory, suppliers, products and drug expiry: $928.5K total sales, 2M units total stock, 525K available stock and $419K revenue planning. Across 12, 18 and 24-month expiry windows, 87,716 units (about 17% of available stock) expire within 12 months, with top at-risk drugs (Tolcapone, Furosemide, Chlorpromazine) flagged for priority clearance. Three agents generate about 89% of sales, and Antifungal is the top category at $0.24M (about 26%).

Healthcare Data Analysis

Analysis of 55,500 healthcare records using Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn). Cleaning, datetime conversion, duplicate removal and billing validation produced a 54,966-record dataset. Length of Stay and month-level features across 61 months revealed billing and admission trends, while 90th-percentile analysis identified 5,497 high-billing records and 6,038 emergency cases with abnormal test results.

SQL · MySQL

Hospital Management System

SQL analysis (MySQL) of a hospital database answering 14 business questions. Found that 5 long-term illnesses make up about 53% of diagnosed visits, 14% of appointments were missed or cancelled, and rooms and beds bring in about 98% of revenue—showing why bed availability matters most.
